Frequently Asked Questions

What does an SEO agency in the Netherlands do?

An SEO agency helps a website become more relevant and accessible for organic searches. Services may include audits, SEO consultancy, keyword research, technical improvements, helpful content, local optimization, link building and reporting.

Can you help businesses targeting customers in Amsterdam?

Yes. We research location-specific opportunities for your target customers and real service area. Our work can be delivered remotely; we do not represent ourselves as having an office in that city.

Can you optimize for both Dutch and English searches?

Yes. We research search behavior in each language separately. A literal translation does not always reflect the phrases or intent people use in the Netherlands.

Is local SEO different from national SEO?

National SEO can target broader searches across a country. Local SEO concentrates on searches related to a city or area and may include local pages, business information and Google Business Profile when the business is eligible.

Do you offer an initial SEO audit for free?

Yes. We offer a free first review for a website. It highlights visible priorities and possible next steps; it is not a promise of rankings or a complete paid SEO engagement.

What affects the cost and timing of SEO?

Website condition, competition, business goals and the work required affect cost and timing. After reviewing your situation, we can explain a recommended scope and quote. Search improvements do not have a guaranteed timetable.

How will you measure SEO progress?

We review suitable measures such as Google Search Console impressions, queries and clicks, organic visits, indexing, completed technical fixes and enquiries where tracking is available. We distinguish observed changes from assumptions about their cause.
