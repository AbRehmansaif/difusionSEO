Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about SEO services and working with businesses in Switzerland.

What do SEO services in Switzerland include?

SEO services can include technical checks, keyword research, content optimisation, internal linking, local SEO and relevant authority-building work. The right mix depends on your website, customers and the Swiss regions or languages you want to reach.

What is the difference between an SEO agency and an SEO consultant?

An SEO consultant usually diagnoses issues and guides decisions. An SEO agency may also coordinate content and technical implementation. DifusionSEO can provide strategic advice and agreed implementation support, depending on your project.

Can you work with businesses in Switzerland remotely?

Yes. Our team supports Switzerland-focused businesses remotely, agreeing on access, communication, scope and reporting before a project begins.

Do you offer SEO services for Geneva and Zurich?

Yes, we can research search demand and plan optimisation for businesses serving Geneva, Zurich and other Swiss areas. Local pages should reflect genuine operations and useful regional information, not artificial location claims.

Do Swiss websites need German, French and Italian SEO pages?

Not necessarily. The right language choices depend on your customers and resources. If you serve different language markets, separate, properly localised pages can be more useful than a single page filled with translated keywords. Language annotations are appropriate only for actual alternate versions.

How much do SEO services cost in Switzerland?

There is no single price for every website. Cost depends on your existing site, competition, languages, technical issues and whether you need advice, implementation or ongoing support. After an initial review, we can explain a realistic scope before you commit.

How long does SEO take to improve rankings?

There is no fixed timeline. Some technical changes can be checked soon after implementation, while competitive keywords and content improvements may take longer to assess. We cannot guarantee rankings, traffic growth or a specific completion date.

What is included in the free initial SEO audit?

The initial audit is a high-level review of visible search and website issues, followed by practical next-step suggestions. Deeper crawling, detailed competitor research and implementation are defined separately if a project proceeds.
