Answers for UK businesses

Frequently Asked Questions About UK SEO Services

What do SEO services for UK businesses include?

SEO services usually combine technical website checks, keyword research, content improvements, internal linking and authority-related work. The right mix depends on your site, competition, customers and goals. Local businesses may also need Google Business Profile support.

What is the difference between an SEO consultant and an SEO agency?

SEO consultants typically assess problems, advise on priorities and guide decisions. An agency may also coordinate technical changes, content and ongoing delivery. DifusionSEO provides consultancy and implementation support according to the scope agreed with your team.

Do you work with businesses across the United Kingdom?

Yes. We work with UK businesses remotely, including businesses serving customers nationally and those focused on a local area. We do not claim a physical UK office simply because we provide services to the UK market.

Can you improve an existing website without rebuilding it?

Often, yes. We first assess content, indexing, technical health and internal links. Many improvements can be made within the current website. If you also need design or development, we scope it separately and explain why a rebuild would—or would not—be useful.

Do you provide local SEO for UK businesses?

Yes. Local SEO work can include accurate business information, eligible Google Business Profiles, relevant location pages, local keyword research and monitoring. Visibility can vary by the searcher's location, device and competition.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO results vary. A technical fix can be implemented quickly, but its effect on search performance may take longer to assess. Competitive keywords and new content often require sustained work. We cannot guarantee a particular ranking or completion date.

How much do your SEO services cost?

Pricing depends on the website, competition and work required. After an initial discussion and review, we can outline priorities and a proposed scope so you can evaluate the work before committing.

What is included in the free SEO audit?

The free initial audit is a high-level review of visible website and search issues, with suggested next steps. It is not a promise of a complete forensic crawl, competitor investigation or implementation before a project is agreed.
